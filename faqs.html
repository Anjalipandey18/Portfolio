    FAQs

    How do I know the status of my order?

    Edelprime Team keeps you informed at every stage of your order journey. You can check your order status through the following channels:

    • WhatsApp & Email Updates: You will receive order-related notifications and updates on the WhatsApp number and email address provided at the time of purchase.
    • My Orders: Log in to your account on www.edelprime.com, go to the My Orders section and select your order to view its current status.
    • Shipment Tracking: Once your order has been dispatched, use the tracking link provided to follow your shipment.
    When will I receive my tracking information?

    Your shipment tracking details will be shared via email, SMS and/or WhatsApp once your order has been successfully dispatched.

    You can use the tracking link to monitor your shipment's progress and stay updated on its estimated delivery. Please allow some time for the tracking information to become active after dispatch.

    Where can I find my tracking number?

    Your tracking number will be shared through your shipment confirmation email and/or WhatsApp message once your order has been dispatched. Alternatively, you can find your tracking details by following these steps:

    • Visit www.edelprime.com.
    • Log in to your registered account.
    • Navigate to the My Orders section.
    • Select your order to view the available shipment and tracking information.
    Why isn't there any update on my tracking link?

    Tracking information may take approximately 24–48 hours after shipment dispatch to become available or reflect the latest updates in the courier's tracking system.

    If your tracking information remains unchanged after this period, please contact our Customer Care team:

    • General: info@edelprime.com
    • Customer Care: care@edelprime.com

    Our team will be happy to assist you with your shipment-related concerns.

    My tracking status says "Delivered," but I haven't received the package. What should I do?

    If your order is marked as Delivered but you have not received the package, we recommend checking with your family members, neighbours or building reception/security desk, as the package may have been received on your behalf.

    If you are still unable to locate your order, please contact our Customer Care team immediately so we can assist you in investigating the delivery status.

    Customer Care Email: care@edelprime.com

    Are your products BPA-free?

    BPA (Bisphenol A) is an industrial chemical commonly associated with certain plastics and resins. Please refer to the individual product description and specifications for detailed material information.

    What does BPA-free mean?

    BPA-free means that the product does not contain Bisphenol A, a chemical commonly found in plastics that may pose health risks. BPA-free products are safer for food and drink use.

    What are insulated flasks?

    Edelprime Team offers insulated drinkware designed to help maintain the temperature of beverages for extended periods. An insulated flask generally features a double-wall construction with an inner and outer wall separated by a vacuum-insulated space. This vacuum layer minimizes heat transfer between the inside and outside surfaces, helping beverages stay hot or cold for longer. Our insulated stainless steel flasks are designed to combine functionality, durability and everyday convenience.

    Are Edelprime flasks suitable for both hot and cold beverages?

    Yes. Edelprime insulated stainless steel flasks are designed to accommodate both hot and cold beverages. Their double-wall vacuum insulation helps maintain beverage temperatures for extended periods, making them suitable for everyday hydration, travel and outdoor use. For best results, follow the care and usage instructions provided with your product.
