    Privacy Policy

    Edelprime Team recognises the importance of protecting your privacy and values the trust you place in us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you visit or use https://www.edelprime.com (the "Website"), contact our team, or place an order through the Website.

    This Policy applies to current and former visitors of the Website and customers who interact with Edelprime through the Website or the contact channels listed below. By accessing or using the Website, you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law for a particular processing activity, we will seek that consent separately. The Website is operated under the Edelprime brand. For privacy-related questions, please use the contact details in the "Contact Us" section.

    1. Information We May Collect

    a. Contact information

    We may collect information such as your name, email address, mobile or telephone number, delivery address, billing address, city, state, PIN code, country and other contact details you choose to provide.

    b. Order and transaction information

    When you place an order, we may collect information relating to the products ordered, order value, order history, delivery instructions, billing details, payment status, refunds, returns, replacements and customer-service interactions.

    c. Payment information

    Payments may be processed by third-party payment service providers. Edelprime does not intend to collect or store complete card numbers, card security codes or other full payment credentials on its own Website systems. The payment provider may collect and process payment information in accordance with its own terms and privacy policy. The payment methods and provider available to you will be shown at checkout.

    d. Information you post or submit

    If Website features allow you to submit a review, comment, photograph, enquiry or other content, we may collect the information you choose to submit. Information posted in a public area may be visible to other users. Please do not submit personal or confidential information that you do not wish to make public.

    e. Demographic and preference information

    We may collect demographic information or preferences that you voluntarily provide, for example through a survey, feedback form, product enquiry or other interaction.

    f. Technical and usage information

    When you use the Website, we or our service providers may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location derived from technical data, pages viewed, referring or exit pages, access times and interactions with Website features. The precise information collected depends on the Website configuration and the tools in use.

    g. Cookies and similar technologies

    The Website may use cookies, pixels, tags, local storage or similar technologies to support essential Website functions, remember preferences, understand Website usage, improve performance and, where enabled and permitted, measure or deliver advertising. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and any cookie controls made available on the Website. Disabling certain cookies may affect Website functionality.

    2. How We Collect Information

    a. Directly from you

    We collect information when you create an account (if this feature is available), place an order, submit a form, request support, contact us by email or other listed channels, participate in a survey, or voluntarily submit a review or other content.

    b. Automatically through the Website

    We and our service providers may use server logs, cookies and similar technologies to collect technical and usage information when you browse or interact with the Website. Analytics or advertising tools will be used only to the extent they are configured on the Website and permitted by applicable law.

    c. From service providers and other third parties

    We may receive information from service providers involved in payment processing, delivery, website hosting, customer support, fraud prevention or analytics. If you interact with a third-party social media or other integrated feature, that provider may share information with us in accordance with your settings and its privacy policy. We do not assume that any particular integration is available unless it is enabled on the Website.

    3. How We Use Your Information

    Edelprime Team may use information for the following purposes, as relevant to your interaction with us:

    • To receive, verify, process, fulfil and deliver orders, and to manage payments, returns, replacements and refunds.
    • To communicate with you about orders, delivery updates, account activity, product enquiries, complaints and customer support.
    • To respond to your questions, requests and feedback.
    • To provide personalisation or other services you specifically request, and to confirm the details you submit for such services.
    • To maintain, operate, secure and improve the Website, products and customer experience.
    • To understand Website usage, customer interests and service performance, including through analytics tools where configured.
    • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, unauthorised activity, security incidents and misuse of the Website.
    • To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes and lawful requests from public authorities.
    • To send marketing communications about products, launches, offers or promotions where permitted by law and, where required, with your consent.

    We may combine information collected through different interactions where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy and permitted by law.

    4. How We Share Information

    We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

    • Service providers: with vendors and service providers who support Website hosting, payment processing, order fulfilment, logistics, delivery, customer support, communications, security, analytics or other business operations.
    • Legal and regulatory requirements: where disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, court order, legal process or a lawful request from a competent authority.
    • Protection and fraud prevention: where reasonably necessary to detect or investigate suspected fraud, protect the rights, property, safety or security of Edelprime, customers or others, or enforce our Website terms.
    • Business transfers: in connection with a proposed or completed merger, restructuring, financing, acquisition or transfer of all or part of the business, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable law.

    Third-party service providers may process information under their own contractual terms and privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies where relevant.

    5. Marketing Communications and Opt-Out

    Where permitted by applicable law, we may send you promotional emails, messages or other marketing communications about new products, launches, offers and updates. You can opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link included in the email, where available, or by contacting us at care@edelprime.com.

    Opting out of marketing communications will not prevent us from sending essential service or transactional communications relating to an order, payment, delivery, return, refund, security matter or customer-support request. We will process opt-out requests within a reasonable period, subject to applicable law and operational requirements.

    6. Data Security and Retention

    We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including order administration, customer support, accounting, legal compliance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and enforcement of agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable legal requirements. When information is no longer required, we will take steps to delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable law.

    7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

    Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of processing, you may have rights to request access to, correction or updating of, or deletion of certain personal information; withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; or raise a grievance about the handling of your information. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary for legitimate business, security or dispute-resolution purposes.

    To make a privacy-related request, contact care@edelprime.com and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

    8. Third-Party Websites and Services

    The Website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms, payment providers, courier services or other external services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this Policy. Edelprime is not responsible for the privacy, security or content practices of third-party services. Please review their privacy policies before providing information to them.

    9. Children's Privacy

    The Website is intended for users who are legally capable of entering into transactions under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental or guardian consent is required. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact care@edelprime.com so that we can review the matter and take appropriate steps.

    10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    Edelprime Team may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, Website features, service providers or legal requirements. The revised Policy will be posted on the Website with an updated "Last updated" date. Where required by applicable law, we will provide notice of material changes or obtain consent. Please review this page periodically.

    11. Governing Law

    This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India. Any concerns or disputes relating to privacy will be addressed in accordance with applicable Indian law. Nothing in this Policy limits any rights or remedies available to you under applicable data-protection or consumer-protection laws.

    • Order-related enquiries: orders@edelprime.com
